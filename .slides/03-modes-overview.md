## Four modes

- **Ask** — Understand the codebase, bring in external context.
- **Plan** — Research, clarify, then commit to a written plan.
- **Agent** — Execute and iterate across the repo.
- **Debug** — Hypothesize, reproduce, and verify complex bugs at runtime.

Same surface, different **permissions** and **defaults**.
