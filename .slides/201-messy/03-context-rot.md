# What Is Context Rot?

As a conversation grows, **context rot** sets in:

- Early instructions get pushed further from the AI's "attention"
- Conflicting messages accumulate, confusing the model
- The AI starts to drift — ignoring rules, repeating mistakes, losing coherence

This is why you find yourself saying the same thing three times in one session.

**The fix isn't to repeat yourself — it's to manage what's in the window.**
