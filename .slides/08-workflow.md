## When to chain modes

- **Unclear or large change** → **Plan** then **Ask**.
- **Need to read / map the system** → **Ask**.
- **Ready to ship with clear "done"** → **Agent**.
- **Broken or flaky behavior** → **Debug** then **Agent**.

---

*Use **Ask** learning, review, exploring options*

*Use **Plan** the change is big or steps are fuzzy*

*Use **Agent** the change is big or steps are fuzzy*

*Use **Debug** you need to isolate a tricky runtime bug*
