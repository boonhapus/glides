## Cheatsheet | Models

***Use the best model for every task** — switch when the workload changes.*
---
**OpenAI** — Best-in-class at reasoning. Switch to these for **Debug** mode when facing deep logic errors or when **Plan** mode needs to catch obscure edge cases.
---
**Anthropic (Claude)** — Industry standard at coding. Use **Sonnet** for reliable multi-file edits and **Opus** for complex architectural refactors where precision is non-negotiable.
---
**Google (Gemini)** — Unrivaled 1M+ token window. Use **Gemini 3.1 Pro** for **Ask** or **Plan** modes when you need to `@` your entire codebase without losing accuracy.
---
**Cursor** — Prioritize sub-second predictions based on your immediate local file context rather than broad reasoning. Use for **Tab** and **Model** routing.
